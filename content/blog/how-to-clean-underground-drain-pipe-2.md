Underground drain pipes are not visible, yet they should not be overlooked. These pipes are crucial to keeping your house dry, clean, and flood-free. But what happens if they clog? Dealing with this issue may seem frightening, but with the appropriate attitude, it is manageable. This article will teach you how to clean underground drain pipe systems properly, the causes of blockages, and how to avoid them.

## Signs of Blockages in Underground Drain Pipes

You don’t have to be a plumbing expert to know when anything is amiss. Here are several warning signs:

## Slow Draining Water

Is water accumulating around your feet in the shower? Is the sink taking forever to drain? That’s an obvious indicator that something is caught in the underground drain pipe. The slower the drainage, the larger the obstruction.

## Foul Smells Coming from the Drain

If you smell something bad near your drains, do not ignore it. Bad smells frequently indicate that trash and food particles are stuck and rotting somewhere deep in the system.

## Gurgling Sounds from the Drain

When you hear weird gurgling noises emanating from your drains or toilets, they are typically caused by air trapped owing to a blockage. That’s your system begging for assistance.

## Common Causes of Blockages in Underground Drain Pipes

Understanding what causes clogs might help you prevent them. Let’s investigate the most prevalent culprits.

## Tree Roots Invading the Pipes

Roots enjoy water. They slip into even the smallest pipe fractures, grow within, and clog the flow. Over time, they can completely take over your underground drain pipe.

## Food and Grease Accumulation

Pouring grease into the sink is a formula for catastrophe. It hardens inside the pipes, trapping food and debris so that water cannot move through.

## Plastic and Other Debris

Often, flush anything but toilet paper down the toilet? That might be why you’re researching how to clean underground drain pipe systems now.

## Damaged or Broken Pipes

Cracked or collapsed pipes don’t simply stop the flow; they can also cause soil erosion and significant property damage. These are major situations that demand quick attention.

## Soil Shifting and Ground Movement

Changes in ground level or pressure can cause subterranean pipes to misalign or break. If your lawn develops strange dips, your underground drain pipe might be in trouble.

## How to Clean Underground Drain Pipes

Cleaning these pipelines isn’t difficult, but it requires the proper equipment and skills. These are the most effective approaches.

## Methods to Clean Underground Drain Pipes

This procedure is suitable for small to moderate blockages. Force the snake down the drain to break up and remove the clog.

Chemical cleansers can help remove oil and organic buildup. However, use with caution as excessive usage may cause pipe damage.

A drain snake is a flexible, coiled instrument that may be inserted deep into the line. It’s great for figuring out how to clean underground drain pipe sections that are hard to reach.

High-pressure water shoots through the obstruction, completely clearing the line. This approach is strong and environmentally friendly.

## Tools and Equipment Needed for Cleaning Underground Drain Pipes

If you’re doing this task yourself, be sure you have the necessary tools.

## Drain Cleaning Machines

These devices use specialized cables to rapidly and efficiently cut through obstructions.

## Drain Snakes and Augers

These are the go-to tools for DIYers learning how to clean underground drain pipe systems without hiring help.

## Hydro Jetting Equipment

Professionals use this system to provide a powerful water stream that clears tough blockages and cleans pipe walls.

## Camera Inspection for Locating Blockages

To know how to find a drain pipe underground, a camera inspection is a must. It identifies the bottleneck without excavating.

## Powerful Augers for Tough Clogs

A motorized auger may be the best choice for removing stubborn garbage or tree roots.

## When to Seek Professional Help for Underground Drain Cleaning

Sometimes it’s best to seek the advice of specialists. Here’s when you should call them:

Persistent Clogs That Can’t Be Cleared

If you’ve tried everything and the blockage persists, it’s time to call in a professional.

Water backing up into your home may lead to mold and structural damage. Don’t wait; act quickly.

Unbearable smells indicate that something is terribly wrong. It might be sewage, which poses a health risk.

## Preventative Measures to Avoid Future Clogs

Prevention is preferable to repairing. Simple habits may save you thousands.

## Regular Inspection and Maintenance

Schedule yearly checkups to spot minor issues before they become major ones.

## Using Drain Guards to Keep Debris Out

A simple drain guard may prevent hair, food, and other debris from entering the pipes.

## Avoiding Grease and Non-Biodegradable Items in Drains

Think before you pour. Grease, wipes, and plastics do not belong in your plumbing system.
