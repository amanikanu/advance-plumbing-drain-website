Tankless water heaters are energy-efficient and long-lasting, but they need regular care. One of the most common questions is, “How often to flush tankless water heater?” It might seem like a small detail, but neglecting this maintenance task can lead to significant problems.

If you’re unsure when or how to flush your unit—or whether it’s even necessary—this guide covers it all.

## Do You Need To Flush a Tankless Water Heater?

Absolutely, yes. Even the best tankless water heaters need flushing. Ignoring this task allows mineral deposits to build up inside your system.

Skipping flushing allows scale and sediment to coat the heat exchanger. This makes the unit work harder and overheat more easily. Over time, efficiency drops, and damage sets in.

## How Do I Know If My Tankless Water Heater Needs To Be Flushed?

White flakes or chalky residue around fixtures? That’s a red flag. It means hard water minerals are circulating through your heater.

Flushing may be overdue if your hot water isn’t as warm or steady as before. Buildup restricts water flow and heat exchange.

When your heater works harder to produce hot water, it consumes more energy. If your bills suddenly spike, it’s time to check your unit.

Knowing how often to flush tankless water heater depends on how much you use it and your water quality.

## General Guidelines Based on Usage and Water Quality

Flush your system every 6 to 9 months. The more hot water you use, the faster the scale builds up.

If your home has hard water, aim to flush it every 6 months. You might also consider a water softener.

If you already treat your water, you can flush once a year to stay safe.

Homes used occasionally or with only one or two occupants can flush every 12 to 18 months.

## How Often Should I Flush My Tankless Water Heater If I Have A Water Softener?

With soft water, flushing once a year is usually enough. Still, check the manual or consult your installer.

## Should I Flush My Tankless Water Heater After 5 Years?

Yes, especially if it hasn’t been flushed before. Five years of buildup can significantly reduce performance.

## Should You Flush A Water Heater That Has Never Been Flushed?

Definitely, it’s better late than never. Even older units benefit from flushing—it may extend their lifespan.

## How To Flush A Tankless Water Heater

If you’ve been wondering how to flush a tankless water heater, here’s a simple overview.

## Turn Off Power and Water Supply

Shut off the gas or electricity to the unit. Also, the water valves must be closed to stop flow during the process.

Attach hoses from your flush kit to the service ports. Make sure the hoses are secure to avoid leaks.

Use a descaling solution made for tankless systems. Avoid using vinegar or random cleaners—it could void your warranty.

Depending on the buildup, the pump circulates the solution through the heater for 45 to 60 minutes.

After flushing, run clean water through the system to rinse out any residue. Remove the hoses and restore connections.

## Can I Flush My Own Tankless Water Heater?

Yes, but only if you’re confident working with basic plumbing and have the right tools.

Safety first. If you’re unsure, call a professional.

A neglected system may need inspection and more detailed cleaning.

These signs may point to deeper problems that flushing alone won’t fix.

Some complex units need expert handling. Don’t risk damage or warranty issues.

Many manufacturers require professional services for coverage. Always check your warranty terms.

## Average Flushing Time for Most Units

Flushing usually takes 60 to 90 minutes, including setup and rinsing. The flush itself runs for about 45 minutes.

## Factors That May Affect Flushing Duration

Heavily scaled units may take longer to clean thoroughly.

Larger units or those with higher flow rates might need extra time to ensure proper descaling.

## Install a Pre-Filter or Water Softener

These devices catch minerals and debris before they enter your system. This reduces the need for frequent flushing.

A quick yearly check can catch early signs of buildup, leaks, or part failure.

## Monitor Performance Metrics

Watch your water temperature, pressure, and energy bills. Changes can be early clues that it’s time to flush.

## Should I Flush My Tankless Water Heater After 5 Years?

Yes. Even if it’s never been flushed, doing so now can help restore efficiency and extend its life.

## Should You Flush A Water Heater That Has Never Been Flushed?

Yes, flushing removes harmful buildup and may improve water quality and performance.

## How Often Should I Flush My Tankless Water Heater If I Have A Water Softener?

Once a year is usually enough for softened water. Still, monitor for signs of scaling.

## How Long To Flush A Rinnai Tankless Water Heater?

Rinnai systems typically take about one hour to flush. Always follow the manufacturer’s instructions for best results.

Now that you know how often to flush tankless water heater systems and how to flush a tankless water heater, don’t wait for trouble to start. A little maintenance now can save you a lot of money later.

Whether you’re worried about how long to flush tankless water heater units or wondering, “Do you need to flush tankless water heater systems that are brand new?”, the answer is simple—yes, and it’s worth it.
